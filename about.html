About Me

Ever since I was little, I have always enjoed being creative and finding different ways to express my ideas. I've always been drawn to the idea of putting something together and wanted to see how people responded to it. My introduction to journalism was through my creative writing, but as I got older, it developed into a curiosity about brands and how they interact and influence the world around them.

Coming to Mizzou has given me the opportunity to explore these interests in ways I hadn't before. Through my classes, I've learned that there is so much more to the field than just making something that feels good. Outside of school, I enjoy spending time with my friends and family and making memories with the people who mean the most to me. My creativity also follows me outside of the classroom, where I spend a lot of time taking pictures and doing whatever I can to step outside my typical routine.

I created this portfolio as a way to showcase my creativity, interests, and the work I've done along the way. I hope to continue adding to it as I grow professionally and personally.
